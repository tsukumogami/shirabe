# Exploration Decisions: scope-process-framing

## Round 1

- Blast radius is `/scope` only: the process-is-the-product framing is general,
  but the argumentation asymmetry is this skill's, so the fix stays here rather
  than moving into the shared parent-skill pattern.
- Prose and placement only: mechanism is #320's question. A mechanism finding
  would have to be earned against the prose path rather than assumed beside it.
- The scale mismatch is in scope and gets a lead, rather than being deferred as
  a separate concern.
- The write-target enumeration conflict is investigated and a resolution
  proposed by the exploration, rather than the author handing one down.
- The adversarial demand lead is skipped: the source issue carries the `bug`
  label, which the Phase 1 label pre-gate resolves as an explicit skip.

## Round 2

- Explore further rather than crystallize after round 1: four gaps were opened
  and none of them depended on the author's pending answers, so running them
  first put better evidence under those answers.
- Round 2 leads were scoped to round 1's gaps rather than to new territory,
  plus one lead added from the author's question about whether `/scope`
  already uses koto.
- The sourcing property is dropped as a framing. It cannot hold under either
  dispatch binding, because the pattern hands a child a name and a topic key
  and children source their own upstream. Retained instead: `/scope`'s
  constant-chain promise, which is verifiably true.
- P2 (`default to the lowest ceremony`) is retired as a tension. It is scoped
  to artifact ceremony in the roadmap and plan workflows and is cited zero
  times by `/scope`. Recorded as a reading risk, not a contradiction.
- Mechanism stays out of #331 even though round 2 found a real eval coverage
  gap. The gap is recorded as a finding for other work.

## Terminal

- The exploration is superseded rather than crystallized. The author redirected
  the line of work: the real subject is adopting koto for `/scope` in a way
  that resolves this incident, and that wants its own exploration with the
  right framing rather than a course correction inside this one.
- The research stands and carries forward. Both rounds' findings are the input
  to the reframed exploration, not discarded by it.
- Two constraints are recorded as binding on any koto work, because the
  research falsified the premise they correct: koto buys instruction
  sequencing and gating rather than isolation, and disclosure must defer
  verdicts while front-loading premises.
- The framing content is not superseded by the koto reframe. koto governs when
  a directive arrives, never what it says.
