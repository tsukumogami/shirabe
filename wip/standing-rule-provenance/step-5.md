### Step 5: --rules says for every row whether it grants, from the floor, the repository anchor and niwa authority check, and a row never widens the session's grant

**Goal**: `rule_grants` lands in `state-schema.jq`, and `--rules` prints `grants` and `allows` on every Standing object. shirabe keeps two expectations about row text and reads the allowance from the authority work's existing read; it builds no grant model of its own. The floor: a pause, an inferred row, a `pinned` row, an unreadable or superseded row, and a row whose cited decision has an `asserted` reversal never grant. The repository anchor: only a `skill` row citing the repository the installed plugin's manifest names as its source (the `repository` field of `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` under the plugin root, read as `https://github.com/<owner>/<repo>`; a manifest naming none anchors nothing), and a `doc` row citing the record's host repository, can carry a grant. The anchor stays shirabe's provenance rule about whose merged words a row may quote, not a grant model, and the authority read doesn't replace it. A row the floor or the anchor settles prints `grants` false and `allows` `[]` and never causes a read. For the rest, `--rules` makes at most one read per invocation, `niwa authority check -C <instance> --session <id> --json`, and only when at least one row among those it prints passes both; that one answer applies to every such row. The instance is the first directory at or above the working directory holding `.niwa/instance.json`, made canonical, the walk `posture-read.sh` makes for its own grant read. The session is `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID`, the coordinator's Claude session, which niwa ties to its dispatch. `record/host` stores neither, and the record's one session fact is `record/meta`'s driver, which a takeover writes, so when `record/meta` names a driver other than `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` the caller isn't the session driving this record and no read is made. niwa is found on PATH, else as an executable `$NIWA_BIN`, and the read is bounded at `NIWA_LOOKUP_SECONDS` (3 seconds), as `posture-read.sh` finds and bounds it. `grants` is true only for a row in effect that passes the floor and the anchor, when the read exited 0 and printed a `niwa.authority.check/v1` document with `"state": "bound"`, a `grant` array whose every `authority` is `merge-pr`, `close-issue` or `cut-release`, and a `level` of `autonomous`, `supervised` or `strict`. `allows` is then that grant's authorities in niwa's order, which may be empty. Any other exit, state or shape, no niwa, no session id, no instance root, or a read past its bound gives every row `grants` false and `allows` `[]`, and `--rules` still exits 0. Rows restrict and never widen: `allows` is the session's grant as the read answers it, never more, whatever a row's words say. No `--point` is passed, no script gates a step on `grants` in this milestone, and the handover read makes no read. No new Standing shape is written and no koto decision command is called.
